**Question:** What are chargebacks?\
**Feedback:** Chargebacks generally mean the reversal of payment for a failed transaction or request for repayment in a transaction previously settled or remitted.\
         A chargeback is raised when a customer pays for a product or service but does not receive value for that service, yet their bank account has been debited for that service. Kindly find more insight on chargeback [here](/docs/charge-backs).
