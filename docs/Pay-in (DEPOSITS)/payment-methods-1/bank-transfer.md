Pay with Transfer is a payment method that involves a one-time payment into a Pooled Accounts or Temporary virtual accounts. Customers can pay into these accounts through their online banking applications. You can access the Pay with Transfer payment method on [Fincra Checkout](https://docs.fincra.com/docs/checkout-redirect) and via API.
