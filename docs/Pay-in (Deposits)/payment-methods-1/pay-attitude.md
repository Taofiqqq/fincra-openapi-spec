Pay with Payattitude is another payment method available on Fincra. This method requires customers to validate the transaction amount and currency,  select Pay with Payattitude, enter their phone number, and authenticate the transaction through the Payattitude app, bank app, or USSD. Easy peasy!

![](https://files.readme.io/49207b6-Pay_Attitude-1.png "Pay Attitude-1.png")

## Supported Banks

Nigerian customers with accounts at any of Access Bank, Fidelity Bank, First Bank, Heritage Bank, Hope PSBank, Sterling Bank, Suntrust Bank, UBA, Zenith Bank, EcoBank, and Accion Bank can pay you with the Pay with PayAttitude option.

You can add Pay with Payattitude to your application, website, or e-commerce store in the following ways:

* Payment Links
* Checkouts
* API
