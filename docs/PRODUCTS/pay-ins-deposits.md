# Accept Global Payments Seamlessly with Fincra Payin

Fincra’s Payin enables you to collect payments from customers around the world with ease, helping you streamline your payment processes.

## What Are Pay-Ins?

Pay-Ins are inbound payments received directly into your merchant account. Fincra offers a comprehensive suite of payment solutions, allowing you to build exceptional products and services while delivering a superior customer experience.

Whether you're a seasoned developer or new to payment solutions, Fincra simplifies the payment process. With just a few clicks, you can generate a payment link from your portal and share it with your customers to start receiving payments instantly—no technical expertise required.

For businesses with specific needs, Fincra’s Payin methods allows for deeper integration into your existing systems. If you require a custom payment flow, we recommend integrating the API into your application. Some technical knowledge of REST APIs and programming languages will be helpful to make the most of this feature.

### How to accept payments

Our collection service comprises products and features that enable you to receive payments quickly. You can accept payments in a variety of ways, including:

* Checkout via [Checkout Redirect](checkout-redirect-1) and [Checkout Pop up](checkout-pop-up)
* [Direct Charge](direct-charge-api-1)
* [Direct Debit](direct-debit-api-1)
* [Virtual Account](virtual-account-api)
* [Payment Links](payment-links-no-code)
* [Multicurrency Account](multicurrency-virtual-account-1)
