As a service provider, it's crucial to adhere to international regulations and compliance standards. We've established strict guidelines to ensure the security and legality of our services. Our Multicurrency Virtual Account (MCY) solution facilitates seamless transactions across multiple currencies. However, to maintain our platform's integrity and trustworthiness, we prohibit certain activities and restrict access from specific countries.

We're committed to providing a secure and compliant financial environment. This commitment involves preventing illicit activities such as money laundering, terrorist financing, and fraud. To this end, we continuously monitor and update our policies in line with global regulatory frameworks and best practices.
