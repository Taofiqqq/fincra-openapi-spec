In addition to SDKs, we offer plugins that allow for easy integration with popular e-commerce platforms. These plugins are designed specifically for merchants who want to embed our payment solutions into their existing platforms without writing extensive code. Our plugins are particularly useful for users of platforms like WooCommerce, allowing you to start accepting payments quickly and efficiently.

#### Available Plugins:

1. **WooCommerce Plugin (V1 & V2)**\
   Our WooCommerce plugin provides merchants with a simple and secure way to accept payments on their WooCommerce store. It integrates seamlessly with the checkout process, allowing customers to pay via various methods such as card payments, mobile money, and bank transfers.  
   * **WooCommerce V1 Plugin**
   * **WooCommerce V2 Plugin**

2. **ForGive Plugin**\
   Designed for donation platforms, the ForGive plugin enables seamless donations through various payment channels. It integrates easily with charity websites, allowing users to contribute to causes without leaving the site. The plugin supports recurring donations, custom amounts, and donation tiers, making it ideal for non-profit organizations.

3. **PaymentForms Plugin**\
   The PaymentForms plugin allows merchants to embed customizable payment forms on their websites. This is ideal for businesses that want more control over the checkout process while keeping the integration simple. The plugin supports multiple currencies, custom fields, and full design customization to match the look and feel of your website.
