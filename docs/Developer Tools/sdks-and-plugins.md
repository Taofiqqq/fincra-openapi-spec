Whether you're building a mobile app, a web application, or integrating with a popular e-commerce platform, we've got SDKs and plugins tailored for your specific needs. Each solution is designed to streamline the integration process, allowing you to focus on building great products while leveraging our powerful payments infrastructure.

**[SDKs](sdks)** are ideal for developers who need full control over the integration process. If you are building a custom solution and want to control every aspect of payment processing, refunds, and transaction tracking, SDKs offer the flexibility you need.

On the other hand, **[Plugins](plugins)** are perfect for businesses that want a quick and easy integration with existing platforms like WooCommerce. They offer the fastest way to start accepting payments without needing extensive development work, making them ideal for e-commerce sites and non-profit organizations.
