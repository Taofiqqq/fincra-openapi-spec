Customers can pay the agent by bank transfer using the Pay with Transfer option. The agent gives their customers the POS machine's bank account information, and the customers make a transfer to the bank account.

The majority of customers do bank transfers using their bank's mobile app. When they do, the transaction is automatically approved, and the amount paid appears on the POS machine and agent portal immediately.
