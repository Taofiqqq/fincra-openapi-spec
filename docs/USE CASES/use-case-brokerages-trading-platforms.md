#### Faster deposits. Reliable withdrawals. Global access.

Fincra powers trading and brokerage platforms with infrastructure that enables **instant funding, seamless payouts, and trusted FX services**.

***

## The Problem

Trading platforms live or die on **speed and trust**. Without reliable rails:

* Customers face delays in funding their accounts.  
* Withdrawals become slow and unpredictable.  
* FX inefficiencies create poor trading experiences.  

***

## The Fincra Solution

Fincra ensures brokerages and trading platforms can deliver:

* **Instant account funding** through multiple payment channels.  
* **Frictionless withdrawals** at scale.  
* **Global FX coverage** with transparent pricing.  
* **Built-in compliance** to foster user trust.  

***

## What You Can Do

### 1. Enable instant account funding

Make deposits smooth, reliable, and ready for trading.

* Pay-ins via **bank transfers, cards, and wallets**.  
* **Real-time confirmation** of deposits.  
* Automated **reconciliations** for operators.  

💡 **How it works:** A trader funds their brokerage account in EUR. The deposit clears instantly, and trading begins immediately.

***

### 2. Offer frictionless withdrawals

Ensure users can cash out without delays.

* **Real-time disbursements** to banks and wallets.  
* Support for **bulk payouts** at the platform level.  
* Transparent **end-to-end tracking**.  

💡 **How it works:** A trading platform processes thousands of user withdrawals daily. With Fincra, payouts are automated and delivered in seconds.

***

### 3. FX for a global audience

Support global users with multi-currency solutions.  

* Accept deposits in **USD, EUR, GBP, CAD**.  
* Convert instantly to **NGN, KES, GHS, and more**.  
* Access **deep liquidity** for large settlements.  

💡 **How it works:** A user deposits USD but trades in NGN. Fincra handles the conversion instantly and at competitive rates.
