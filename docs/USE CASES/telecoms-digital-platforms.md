Fincra enables telecoms and digital platforms to **collect subscriptions, pay agents and partners, and manage FX across borders** — all via one API.

## The Problem

Telecoms and digital platforms often face:

- Difficulty collecting recurring payments across diverse channels.  
- Complex disbursements to agents, partners, and networks.  
- FX exposure when settling roaming or cross-border services.  
- Compliance challenges in high-volume digital ecosystems.  

***

## The Fincra Solution

Fincra provides scalable rails for telecoms and platforms to grow:  

- **Subscription collections** via direct debit, cards, mobile money, and bank transfers.  
- **Automated payouts** to agents, vendors, and partners.  
- **FX APIs** for roaming and cross-border settlements.  
- **Compliance-first infrastructure** with AML/KYC built in.  

***

## What You Can Do

### 1. Collect recurring subscriptions

Simplify recurring billing for telecom and digital services.  

- Collect via **direct debit, cards, mobile wallets, and bank transfers**.  
- Enable **international collections** in USD, GBP, EUR, CAD.  
- Fast reconciliation for subscription-based services.  

💡 **How it works:** A SaaS platform collects monthly subscriptions in USD and settles locally in NGN using Fincra’s APIs.

***

### 2. Disburse to agents and partners

Pay large networks of agents and service partners seamlessly.  

- **Real-time payouts** to bank accounts and mobile wallets.  
- **Bulk disbursements** with one API call.  
- Transparent reconciliation and audit trails.  

💡 **How it works:** A telecom operator pays thousands of airtime agents across Kenya and Ghana. Fincra automates payouts instantly, reducing operational overhead.

***

### 3. Manage FX for cross-border services

Handle roaming and global digital settlements efficiently.  

- **Real-time conversions** at competitive FX rates.  
- Access **deep liquidity pools** for large settlements.  
- Avoid hidden charges and double conversions.  

💡 **How it works:** A telco collects NGN from local subscribers but needs to settle roaming partners in USD. Fincra’s FX APIs handle the conversion instantly and transparently.
