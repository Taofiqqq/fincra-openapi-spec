## What You Can Do

Allow players to start betting without delays.  

- Accept deposits via **cards, bank transfers, and mobile wallets**.

- Automated reconciliations for operators.

***

### 2. Offer fast, frictionless withdrawals

Boost player trust with reliable cash-outs.  

- **Instant payouts** to bank accounts and wallets.  
- Support **bulk payouts** for platform operators.  
- Transparent tracking for every withdrawal.  

💡 **How it works:** A betting operator processes thousands of daily withdrawals. With Fincra, payouts are automated and delivered within seconds.

***

### 3. Manage FX for international players

Support global bettors and cross-border settlements.  

- Accept deposits in **USD, GBP, EUR, CAD**.  
- Convert instantly to **NGN, KES, GHS, and more**.  
- Access **deep liquidity pools** for large transactions.  

💡 **How it works:** An international player funds an account in USD. Fincra converts to NGN instantly at competitive rates, enabling local betting.
