One of the many benefits of integrating Fincra into your website, application, or e-commerce store is its ability to help you receive payments from customers from any part of the world and with several payment methods. Such as Cards, Bank accounts, PayAttitude, Mobile Money, and POS terminals.

Payment methods are the number of ways merchants can collect payments from their customers.

Here is a list of the payment channels supported on Fincra;

* [Virtual Accounts](/docs/fincra-virtual-accounts)
* [Cards](/docs/card-1)
* [Bank Transfers](/docs/bank-transfer)
* [Pay Attitude](/docs/pay-attitude)
* [Mobile Money](/docs/mobile-money-api)
* [POS Terminal](/docs/pos-terminal-overview)
