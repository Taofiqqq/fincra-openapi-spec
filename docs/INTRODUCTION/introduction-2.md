# About Fincra

Fincra Technologies Limited is a payment infrastructure for fintechs, global businesses and platforms. Fincra provides payment solutions that help businesses to accept payments securely, and offer financial services to their customers. 

We are focused on making cross border payments seamless and ensuring that the transfer of value and money across the globe is as easy as sending a text.

Fincra offers various methods for **collections (pay-ins)** and **disbursements (pay-outs)** which you can access via APIs and no-code solutions. In addition to these, you can also utilise our **conversions** and **identity validation services** to trade in several currencies and validate information your customers provide respectively.

Our full suite of products allow businesses to innovate, scale up and provide the best payment offerings to their customers worldwide.

# Onboarding on Fincra

Before you jump right into your integration with Fincra, it's important to note that you would need to successfully onboard on our platform via [app.fincra.com](app.fincra.com) to gain access to the full suite of services outlined above.

Our onboarding serves as way for us to vet your business and to ensure that you're given access to the right products at Fincra.

For a seamless onboarding experience, it is important to have certain documents and details handy. Some of these details include:

1. KYB information that speaks more to your business such as your company address, website, social media pages, operating industry, incorporation number etc.
2. Your business incorporation certificate and other business incorporation documents that can attest to the business owners (e.g directors and shareholding structure / information)
3. KYC information for your business owners so we can vet their identities such as valid government IDs, address information etc.
4. And the relevant supporting documents for all the aforementioned KYB & KYC details.

With these details, we are able to instantly review and verify your business and grant you access to our services.
