A chargeback occurs when a collection is reversed by the payer’s bank after a charge on their account is disputed. A chargeback is raised when a customer pays for a product or service but does not receive value for that service, yet their bank account has been debited for that service.

As a merchant, you can view, accept or reject a chargeback by using the following endpoints : 

1. [List chargebacks  ](/reference/list-chargebacks)

2. [Accept a chargeback](/reference/accept-a-chargeback) 

3. [Reject a chargeback](/reference/reject-a-chargeback)
