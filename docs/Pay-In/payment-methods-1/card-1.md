One of the easiest ways for people to pay online is with a card. As a Fincra merchant, your customers can pay you from anywhere in the world with debit or credit cards. Fincra works well with most credit and debit card schemes,  allowing you to accept credit/debit card payments from your customers.

![3145](https://files.readme.io/8a62dcc-pay-with-cards-1.png "pay-with-cards-1.png")

There are two ways to accept card payments with Fincra :

* Card Payments with [Checkouts](http://google.com)
* Card Payments with [Direct Charge APIs](http://google.com)
