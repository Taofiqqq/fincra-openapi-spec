With Fincra integrated into your website, application, or e-commerce store, your customers can make payments to you with their bank accounts through the following methods:

  * [Checkouts](http://google.com)
  * [Temporary virtual accounts](http://google.com)
  * [Permanent virtual accounts](http://google.com)
  * [Bank transfers via Direct charge APIs](http://google.com)
