## Overview

The refunds API allows you as a merchant to initiate refunds, which can be used in either of the scenarios below:

- When a paying customer made an underpayment and you would like to reverse the full amount back to the payer.
- When a paying customer made an overpayment and you would like to reverse the extra amount back to the payer.

Note, the refund feature is also available on your dashboard. See [demo video](https://8h6y7.r.a.d.sendibm1.com/mk/cl/f/sh/1t6Af4OiGsE8LIjZCp6N82nAJA8OSC/M5BrJKXHxYdd) on how to process refunds from your dashboard.
